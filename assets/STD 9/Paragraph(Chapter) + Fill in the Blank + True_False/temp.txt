

### Paragraph 1  
*Long long ago a Zulu hunter was sitting under a tree. His name was Edie. He saw a large herd of deer grazing in the lush green meadow.*  

1. **Who was Edie and what was he doing?**  
   Edie was a Zulu hunter, and he was sitting under a tree watching the deer.  

2. **Where were the deer? Describe the place where they were found.**  
   The deer were grazing in a lush green meadow, full of fresh and healthy grass.  

3. **What does the word "lush" mean? Use it in your own sentence.**  
   *Meaning:* Rich, healthy, and full of growth.  
   *Sentence:* The forest looked lush after the monsoon rains.  

4. **From which tribe did Edie belong?**  
   Edie belonged to the Zulu tribe.

### Paragraph 2  
*He said to himself, "These deer are really wonderful but I have to run a lot to catch one of them. Can't there be an easy way to get one?" Suddenly, he saw a female cheetah crawling silently closer to the grazing animals. Then, she leapt and caught a deer. After sometime her two young cubs came out of the bush. They all enjoyed their meal.*  

1. **What problem did Edie face in catching deer?**  
   Edie’s problem was that he had to run a lot to catch a deer, which was very difficult.  

2. **How did the cheetah catch the deer? Mention the steps she followed.**  
   - She crawled silently closer to the deer.  
   - Then she leapt suddenly.  
   - Finally, she caught the deer.  

3. **What is the meaning of "crawling silently"? Why did the cheetah crawl silently?**  
   *Meaning:* Moving slowly and quietly without making noise.  
   *Reason:* The cheetah crawled silently so the deer would not notice her and she could attack by surprise.

### Paragraph 3  
*Edie's eyes brightened. They sparkled with an idea. He envied the cubs. "Can I be lucky as these beautiful cubs? They got their food without any effort. Can I have a hunter like their mother?" He thought. He decided to catch a young cub and train it to hunt for him. Then I shall only say, "O my dear, go and kill a deer for me. Ah! My life will be very comfortable then." He was quite pleased with his own idea.*  

1. **What idea came to Edie's mind when he saw the cheetah hunt?**  
   Edie thought of catching a young cheetah cub and training it to hunt deer for him.  

2. **Why did Edie envy the cheetah cubs?**  
   He envied them because they got food without making any effort, while he had to struggle to catch deer.  

3. **Find a word from the paragraph that means "was jealous of".**  
   The word is **envied**.  

4. **Was Edie's plan a good one? Give a reason for your answer.**  
   No, Edie’s plan was not good because wild animals like cheetahs cannot be easily trained, and it could be dangerous for him.
